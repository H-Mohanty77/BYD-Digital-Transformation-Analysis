# BYD Digital Transformation Analysis

A business and data analytics case study exploring how BYD uses digital technologies to improve research and development, manufacturing, marketing, and after-sales service.

## Project Overview

This project examines BYD's digital transformation strategy and the role of data-driven decision-making in its operations. It brings together a case-study review, an Excel analysis workbook, and a presentation summarizing the business challenges, digital solutions, reported outcomes, and remaining risks.

## Business Problem

As BYD expands in the new energy vehicle market, managing product development cycles, manufacturing efficiency, customer targeting, and after-sales support becomes increasingly complex. The case study describes how digital tools can help address these challenges, while also introducing risks involving data security, talent gaps, system compatibility, and dependence on digital infrastructure.

## Project Objectives

- Understand BYD's digital transformation strategy.
- Examine the reported use of digital twins and virtual simulation in R&D.
- Explore IoT-enabled manufacturing and predictive maintenance.
- Review customer segmentation and data-driven marketing.
- Summarize reported outcomes and identify operational risks.
- Communicate findings in a business-focused presentation.

## Areas Covered

### 1. Digital R&D
The source paper describes virtual simulation and digital-twin approaches intended to support product development and reduce reliance on physical testing.

### 2. Smart Manufacturing
The case discusses industrial automation, Internet of Things (IoT) monitoring, flexible production, and predictive maintenance.

### 3. Data-Driven Marketing
The case describes customer relationship management (CRM), RFM-based customer segmentation, targeted communications, and digital product experiences.

### 4. Predictive After-Sales Service
The paper discusses vehicle-condition data and AI-assisted diagnostics to anticipate maintenance needs and support remote fault resolution.

### 5. Risks and Improvement Areas
The analysis considers data security and cross-border compliance, the shortage of employees with combined automotive and AI skills, compatibility between systems, and the operational impact of cloud outages.

## Key Takeaways

The case illustrates how digital systems may connect product development, factory operations, customer engagement, and after-sales service. It also highlights that operational gains need to be considered alongside data governance, cybersecurity, workforce capabilities, and system resilience.
